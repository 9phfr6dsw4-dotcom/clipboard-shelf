## Clipboard Shelf 1.3.0

The app icon now follows your skin, and Classic is the new default skin.

- **The icon follows the skin.** Choose a skin from **⋯ → Skin** and Clipboard Shelf's icon in Finder, Launchpad and Spotlight changes to match. **⋯ → App Icon Follows Skin** turns it off and brings back the usual teal icon. After an update the icon is put back the next time the app starts.
- **Classic is the default skin.** If you were on Lagoon, the old default, Clipboard Shelf moves to Classic once. Any other skin you chose stays, and if you pick Lagoon again it stays too.

Your history, pins and other settings stay as they are.
