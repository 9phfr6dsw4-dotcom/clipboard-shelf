## Clipboard Shelf 1.2.0

A new look, six skins and a new icon.

- **Redesigned.** The shelf now looks like a native Mac window, with a frosted background, a search bar at the top, clearer rows and a Pause button in the footer. It keeps the same size, layout and features.
- **Six skins.** Choose Lagoon (the teal you had), Classic, Grape, Sunset, Meadow or Rose from **⋯ → Skin**. The accent colour, pins and background tint all follow it.
- **Light and dark.** **⋯ → Appearance** follows your Mac (Automatic), or stays Light or Dark.
- **Source app icons.** Each new copy shows the icon and name of the app you copied from. It is on by default, and **⋯ → Show Source App Icons** turns it off. Items copied before this update show the usual text or link icon.
- **New app icon:** a bold clipboard with a gold clip.

Your history, pins, pause setting and recent-item limit stay as they are. Only the name of the app you copied from is saved with a new copy, never its icon, and nothing leaves your Mac.
