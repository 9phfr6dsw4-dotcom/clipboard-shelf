## Clipboard Shelf 1.3.1

Clipboard Shelf's own icon is now blue, to match Classic, the default skin.

- **A blue icon from the start.** The download, the update and a new copy all show the blue Classic icon, so it no longer starts teal and changes after the app opens.
- **Lagoon still has its teal icon.** Choose Lagoon from **⋯ → Skin** and the icon in Finder and Launchpad turns teal, like every other skin.

Your history, pins and settings stay as they are.
