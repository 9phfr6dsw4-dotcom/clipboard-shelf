## Clipboard Shelf 1.1.2

- Clipboard Shelf can now update itself. It checks for a new version once an hour and once when it starts, and installs it when the shelf is closed.
- The ⋯ menu has **Check for Updates…**. When an update is waiting, a row at the top of the shelf offers to install it.
- The app now goes online to check for updates. It still sends nothing about your clipboard.
- Nothing else changes: your history, pins, pause setting and recent-item limit stay as they are.
